Cancellation and refunds

How to cancel a RoboHost plan, when we refund a charge, and how quickly the money reaches you.

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

  • The first 7 days are free and need no card, so there is nothing to cancel during the trial.
  • Cancel any time. Charging stops, and your plan runs to the end of the period you have paid for.
  • We refund in full if you were charged by mistake: twice, after you cancelled, or more than the price you were shown.
  • Approved refunds go back to the way you paid. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show them.

1The free trial

Every RoboHost account starts with a free trial for 7 days from sign-up. It needs no card, and nothing is charged when it ends.

If the trial ends without a plan, publishing to booking sites and Auto-Pilot pause. Your data, your bookings and Nova's drafts stay, and you can choose a plan whenever you like.

2How to cancel

  • In the app: open Billing & Compliance, then Plan & payouts, and choose Cancel subscription. Only the account owner can cancel.
  • By email: write to robohost.co@gmail.com from your account's email address, and we will cancel it for you.

Cancelling stops all future charges. Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for, and then lapses: publishing to booking sites and Auto-Pilot pause, and nothing else is removed. You can subscribe again at any time.

3When we refund

Plans are paid in advance for each period. Because every account gets 7 free days first, we do not refund the unused part of a period when you cancel part-way through it.

We refund in full when:

  • you were charged twice for the same period;
  • you were charged after you had cancelled;
  • you were charged more than the price shown at checkout (we refund the difference);
  • a plan renewed when you meant to cancel, you tell us within 7 days of the charge, and you have not published to a booking site or used Auto-Pilot since it renewed.

If we remove a Live feature your plan relied on, as our Terms of service describe, we refund the unused part of your paid period.

Switching plans

To move between monthly and yearly, or change how many properties you pay for, you check out the new plan, and the old one stops charging once the new one is paid. If you switch part-way through a period you had paid for, write to us and we will refund the unused days of the old plan.

A refund includes the GST charged on the refunded amount.

4Asking for a refund

Email robohost.co@gmail.com from your account's email address within 30 days of the charge. Include the receipt number or Razorpay payment ID from Charges and receipts, under Plan & payouts in the app, and a line on what went wrong.

  • We reply within 2 working days. If we cannot approve a refund, we tell you why.
  • We start an approved refund through Razorpay within 2 working days of approving it. It goes back to the card, UPI account, bank account or wallet you paid with.
  • Banks and card networks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show the money in your account.

5Delivery of the service

RoboHost is software you use online, at app.robohost.co.in. Nothing is shipped. Your account works as soon as you sign up, and a paid plan switches on as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment, usually within a few minutes.

If you have paid and your plan has not switched on within 24 hours, email us. We will switch it on or refund the charge.
