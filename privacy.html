Privacy policy

How RoboHost collects, uses, shares and protects personal data. It covers your data if you are a host, and your guests', tenants' and staff's data when you run your properties with RoboHost.

Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version

  • RoboHost is provided by Iraava Hospitality Private Limited, an Indian company. For guests, tenants and staff, we handle data on behalf of the host who uses RoboHost.
  • We collect what we need to run your properties: your account, your listings and bookings, and the messages you and your guests exchange.
  • We do not sell personal data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Nova, our AI co-host, uses third-party AI models to draft replies. Those providers may not train their models on the data we send.
  • To see, correct or delete your data, email robohost.co@gmail.com. Deletion steps are in section 12.

1Who we are

RoboHost is software for people who run homestays, villas, serviced flats and rentals in India. It is provided by Iraava Hospitality Private Limited ("Iraava", "we", "us"), Cloud-9 Towers, Vaishali, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201010, India.

This policy covers:

  • our website, robohost.co.in, and the RoboHost app at app.robohost.co.in;
  • RoboHost's WhatsApp number and the messages we send and receive on it;
  • the private links we send to guests, tenants and vendors (the guest portal, the vendor portal and agreement signing);
  • the emails we send about your account.

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and its Rules, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules on reasonable security practices and sensitive personal data.

Our role

For your RoboHost account, our website and our waitlist, we decide why and how personal data is used. For that data we are the data fiduciary.

When a host uses RoboHost to manage guests, tenants, cleaners and vendors, the host decides why that data is used, and we process it on the host's instructions as a data processor. If you are one of those people, the host is your first point of contact. Section 11 explains how we help.

2What we collect

If you are a host or part of a host's team

  • Account details: your name, email address, mobile number and role (owner, co-host, property manager or investor). We store your password only as a salted one-way hash.
  • Your properties: addresses, listing text and photos, house rules, check-in instructions, Wi-Fi details, door and lockbox codes, cleaning instructions, and contact details for your staff.
  • Tax and payout details, if you add them: PAN, GSTIN and TAN, and the bank account (account holder, account number and IFSC) that owner payouts go to.
  • Plan and billing: your plan, subscription status, invoices and payment references. You enter card and UPI details on Razorpay's pages, and they never reach our servers.
  • Your work in RoboHost: messages you send, edits you make to Nova's drafts, rates and availability, tasks, agreements and settings.
  • WhatsApp: your WhatsApp number and your chats with Nova, if you link them (section 4).

From the booking sites and services you connect

When you connect a booking site, RoboHost reads your account there on your behalf: your listings, your host profile (name, photo, rating and reviews), reservations, guest messages and earnings. We connect in one of two ways, and you choose which:

  • you sign in on the booking site's own page, and we keep the resulting sign-in session, encrypted; or
  • you connect Airbnb, Booking.com or MakeMyTrip through Channex, our channel-manager partner, by signing in inside Channex's own window.

Some connections ask for your username and password for the site. If you give them to us, we store them encrypted and use them only to keep that connection working. Logging out of a site inside RoboHost deletes the saved sign-in.

If you connect Urban Company, Snabbit or MagicBricks, we act in those accounts in the same way. For example, we book a cleaning at your property's address.

About guests, on the host's behalf

  • name and phone number;
  • booking details: dates, number of guests, price, booking site and booking reference;
  • the messages and attachments guests send, on a booking site, on WhatsApp or in the guest portal;
  • for foreign nationals, the details Indian law requires for Form C: nationality, passport and visa numbers, visa type, and date and port of arrival in India. Passport and visa numbers are stored encrypted;
  • invoices and payment status;
  • notes Nova writes when it screens a booking request, and gate-pass details for gated societies.

Guests see their stay details, check-in information and, during their stay, the door code through a private link the host sends them.

About tenants, for long-term rentals

Name, phone number, email address, Aadhaar number (stored encrypted and shown only as its last four digits), lease terms, rent invoices and maintenance requests. When a tenant accepts an agreement electronically, we record the name they typed, the time and their IP address.

About cleaners, vendors and other staff

Name, phone number, email address, skills, working days and hours, languages, notes the host adds, and the messages, photos and costs on the jobs they are given. Vendors open their jobs through a private link and do not need an account.

If you visit our website or join the waitlist

  • Waitlist: your name, email address, mobile number, city, number of homes, the booking sites you use, your listing link if you add one, your referral code and who referred you, the campaign tags in the link you arrived from, the page that sent you, your IP address and your browser type.
  • Free listing check and Nova Playground: the listing link and whatever you type or upload. We send it to our AI provider to produce your answer, and we don't save it to any account. Answers stay in our server's memory for up to 24 hours, and we hold your IP address in memory to limit how often the tools can be used.
  • Chat on a direct-booking page: the messages you type. We send them to our AI provider to answer you, and we don't store them.
  • Sign-ups: when you create an account or accept a team invitation, we note your IP address and browser type to spot fake sign-ups.

Technical data

Our servers log requests (IP address, time, address requested and browser type) so we can keep the service secure and fix problems.

3How we use it

  • To provide RoboHost: syncing calendars, bookings and rates; drafting and sending the messages you approve; scheduling cleaning; preparing GST invoices, Form C and owner statements; and making payouts.
  • To run Nova, our AI co-host (section 5).
  • To keep accounts secure: sign-in, password resets, rate limits, and fraud and abuse checks.
  • To talk to you: account emails (welcome, password reset and security notices), the alerts you switch on, and support. If you joined the waitlist, we write to you only about RoboHost's launch and your place in line.
  • To tell our team about new sign-ups, so we can help you get set up.
  • To meet legal obligations: tax and GST records, Form C under immigration law, and lawful requests from authorities.

We process your data with your consent, which you give when you sign up, join the waitlist or connect an account, and for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows, such as meeting a legal obligation. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 11). Withdrawing does not undo processing already done, and we may then be unable to provide some or all of the service.

We do not sell personal data. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't build advertising profiles.

4WhatsApp

RoboHost sends and receives WhatsApp messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), which Meta provides.

  • Hosts: when you link your WhatsApp number, we send a one-time code to confirm it. We then store your number, your alert preferences and your chat with Nova. You can ask Nova about your bookings and give it instructions, and we send you the alerts you have switched on, such as new bookings and urgent guest messages.
  • Guests: when a guest messages a property on WhatsApp through RoboHost, their messages and any photos they share go to the host, and Nova drafts a reply.
  • Staff, vendors and tenants: we send them cleaning jobs, work orders, gate passes and rent reminders on the host's behalf.

From WhatsApp we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, the content of the messages and media you send us, and delivery and read receipts.

We use this data only to provide the service described above. We don't use WhatsApp data for advertising and we don't sell it. We share it only with the host the conversation belongs to and with the providers in section 6. For example, a message's text goes to our AI provider so Nova can draft a reply. Meta handles messages as the WhatsApp provider under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

To stop receiving messages, tell us on WhatsApp or by email. Hosts can also switch alerts off in RoboHost. To delete your WhatsApp data, see section 12.

5Nova and AI

Nova is RoboHost's AI co-host. It uses large language models from Anthropic (Claude), and we may also use Google's Gemini models. To do its job, Nova sends these providers:

  • guest messages and the conversation so far;
  • property information, house rules and listing text;
  • examples of your earlier replies and the edits you made to its drafts;
  • listing photos, when you ask for a photo check;
  • booking-confirmation emails you forward to RoboHost, so it can read the booking details;
  • your questions on WhatsApp, with a summary of your bookings and revenue, so it can answer them.

We don't send passport or visa numbers to AI providers.

We use these providers under business terms that do not let them train their models on the data we send. They keep it only for as long as those terms allow, for example to monitor for abuse.

We don't train AI models on your data. Nova learns your style by keeping examples of your replies and corrections in your account and including them when it drafts for your properties.

By default, every reply Nova drafts waits for your approval. If you switch on Auto-Pilot for a property, Nova can send some replies on its own. When Nova screens a booking request, its view is advice, and the host decides. AI can make mistakes, so check its drafts.

7Where it is stored

We store RoboHost's data in India, on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai. Some of the providers in section 6 process data outside India, mainly in the United States. The DPDP Act allows this except to countries the Government of India restricts, and we will follow any such restriction.

8How we protect it

  • Connections to robohost.co.in and app.robohost.co.in use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as salted one-way hashes (PBKDF2-SHA256), so nobody at RoboHost can read them.
  • Booking-site sign-ins and logins, passport and visa numbers, and tenant Aadhaar numbers are encrypted in our database.
  • App sessions expire after 12 hours. Password-reset links work once and expire after an hour. Links to invoices and documents expire.
  • Card and UPI payments happen on Razorpay, and we never see card numbers.
  • Only staff who need it can access personal data. Viewing a stored bank account, passport or visa number, or booking-site sign-in takes an administrator, and every such view is logged.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9How long we keep it

  • Your account and property data: while your account is open. When you close it, we delete it within 30 days, except for the records below.
  • Invoices, payments and tax records: up to 8 years, as Indian tax and company law requires.
  • Form C and other records about foreign guests: for as long as immigration law requires the host to keep them.
  • Guest, tenant and staff data: for as long as the host keeps it in RoboHost. It is deleted with the host's account.
  • Waitlist entries: until 12 months after launch, unless you create an account or ask us to delete yours sooner.
  • Server logs: for a limited period, for security and troubleshooting.

10Cookies and browser storage

RoboHost does not set cookies, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The app and website save a few items in your browser's local storage so they work:

  • your sign-in token, so you stay signed in;
  • your light or dark theme;
  • which role you are viewing RoboHost as;
  • your waitlist place and referral code, so you can see them again;
  • a listing link you checked, so you can import it after you sign up;
  • a vendor's name on the vendor portal.

Clearing your browser's site data removes them. Google Fonts, and the Channex panel shown inside the app, may set cookies of their own under their own policies.

11Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and the names of those we have shared it with;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected, completed or updated;
  • have your data deleted, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw consent you have given us, as easily as you gave it;
  • have a grievance about how we handle your data resolved; and
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, email robohost.co@gmail.com from the address on your account, or tell us enough to identify you. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are a guest, tenant, cleaner or vendor of a host who uses RoboHost, please contact the host first, since they decide how your data is used. If you write to us instead, we will pass your request to the host and help them answer it.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

12Deleting your data

  • Hosts: email robohost.co@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my RoboHost account". We will confirm, then delete your account, properties, messages, saved booking-site sign-ins, WhatsApp link and chat history within 30 days. We keep only the records section 9 says we must.
  • Booking sites: you can disconnect at any time. Logging out of a site inside RoboHost deletes its saved sign-in.
  • WhatsApp: email us with your WhatsApp number, and we will delete your WhatsApp messages and unlink the number.
  • Waitlist: email us from the address you signed up with, and we will remove your entry.
  • Guests, tenants, cleaners and vendors: ask the host, or email us and we will help.

13Children

RoboHost accounts are for adults. We don't knowingly let anyone under 18 create an account or join the waitlist. Children can appear in booking details, such as the number of guests or a Form C entry. Hosts should collect only what the booking or the law requires.

14Changes to this policy

When our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell hosts by email or in the app before it takes effect.
