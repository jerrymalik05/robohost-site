Every new account gets a free trial for 7 days from sign-up. It needs no card, and nothing is charged when it ends.

After the trial, you need a plan to publish listings to booking sites and to run Auto-Pilot. Reading your bookings and messages and getting Nova's drafts keep working without one, and nothing you have set up is deleted.

Plans and prices

Both plans include every feature. Prices are per property, before GST:

  • Monthly: ₹2,999 a month, charged every month.

  • We may change our prices. We will tell you by email or in the app at least 30 days before a new price applies to you, and it applies from your next renewal. A period you have already paid for is never re-priced.
